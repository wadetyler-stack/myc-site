# Muddy York voice guide (draft)

Source: 130 Instagram captions (2017–2025) and the old website. Use it to keep site copy sounding like Dylan's crew, not an agency.

## Keep
- **First person plural, owner-voiced.** "We took down some dividing walls..." Plain and practical.
- **Name the work.** Captions list scope item by item ("new electrical, spray foam, hardwood, custom kitchen"). Do the same on project and service pages; specifics are what search and AI answers pick up.
- **Start from what the homeowner wanted.** "Homeowners wanted a new look and to maximize storage so we gave them what they needed."
- **Quiet pride, light humour.** "From rusty mold to white gold." "The last 20% takes 80% of the time — we aim to break that rule." "A little tricky but managed to sort it out."
- **Canadian English.** washroom (alongside bathroom for search), colour, neighbour.
- **Craft details when they're real.** Coped inside corners, resilient channel for sound, re-accessing the main stack. These build trust with homeowners who've been burned before.

## Drop
- Emoji (especially 🤩), "Happy Monday" openers, hashtags.
- Caption typos ("Updates this beautiful washroom", "Contact us for you home").
- Generic agency phrasing: "elevate", "transform your space", "dream home".

## Patterns for the site
- Project card: *What they wanted* → *What we did* (scope list) → *How it turned out* (one line).
- Service page intro: one sentence on the problem, one on how they handle it, then proof (projects, photos, process).
- CTA: "Tell us about your project" / "Book a free consultation". Not "Contact us for your renovation needs".

## Gaps the captions don't cover
Timelines, how pricing works, warranty, permits, who's on the crew. Get these from a 20-minute call with Dylan, and write that copy from what he says.
